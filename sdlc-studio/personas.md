# User Personas

Personas for **inchagram** - a simplified photo-sharing social media platform. These personas represent the target users and guide feature design and user story creation.

**Last Updated:** 2026-01-30
**Status:** Active

---

## Alex - The Photography Enthusiast

**Role:** Amateur Photographer
**Age:** 28
**Technical Proficiency:** Intermediate
**Primary Goal:** Share photography work and discover inspiring visual content without distractions

### Background
Alex is a passionate amateur photographer who shoots landscapes and street photography on weekends. They have a full-time job as a graphic designer but photography is their creative outlet. Alex is frustrated with Instagram's algorithm-driven feed, Stories, Reels, and constant push for engagement metrics. They just want a platform that puts the focus on photos themselves.

### Needs & Motivations
- **Clean viewing experience:** Feed that shows photos in chronological order without algorithmic interference
- **Focus on visuals:** No clutter from comments, engagement bait, or video content
- **Curate their own feed:** Follow photographers whose work they admire
- **Simple sharing:** Upload photos quickly without pressure to add hashtags, captions, or Stories
- **Quality over quantity:** Platform that values good photography over viral content
- **Mindful consumption:** Spend less time scrolling, more time appreciating good photos

### Pain Points
- **Social media fatigue:** Overwhelmed by complex features (Stories, Reels, Shopping, DMs)
- **Algorithm anxiety:** Never sure if followers see their posts
- **Engagement pressure:** Feels forced to post frequently to stay "relevant"
- **Comment spam:** Dealing with bot comments and low-effort engagement
- **Video overload:** Frustrated by platforms pushing video content over photos
- **Feature creep:** Platforms keep adding features nobody asked for

### Typical Tasks
- Upload 2-3 photos per week from recent photo walks
- Browse feed in the morning with coffee to see what photographers they follow posted
- Like photos that inspire them or show technical mastery
- Discover new photographers through explore feed
- Update profile with best recent work

### User Journey
1. **Discovery:** Hears about inchagram from photography forum as "Instagram for people tired of Instagram"
2. **Onboarding:** Creates account, uploads profile photo, writes short bio about photography interests
3. **Exploration:** Uses discover feed to find photographers with similar style
4. **Engagement:** Follows 20-30 photographers whose work resonates
5. **Contribution:** Uploads curated selections from photo walks
6. **Routine:** Checks feed 1-2 times daily for new photos, likes work they admire

### Quote
> "I just want to share my photos and see what other photographers are creating, without all the noise. No videos, no comments asking 'where is this?' - just pure photography."

### Feature Priorities
- **Must Have:** Photo upload, chronological feed, follow/unfollow, likes
- **Should Have:** Discover feed, profile customization
- **Won't Use:** Comments (if added), direct messaging, Stories/Reels

## Jamie - The Casual Sharer

**Role:** Social Media User
**Age:** 22
**Technical Proficiency:** High (digital native)
**Primary Goal:** Share life moments with friends in a simple, low-pressure way

### Background
Jamie is a recent college graduate working their first job. They grew up with social media but increasingly find platforms like Instagram and TikTok exhausting. They like sharing photos of travel, food, and hanging out with friends, but don't want the pressure of maintaining a "perfect feed" or keeping up with trends. Jamie values authenticity and simplicity.

### Needs & Motivations
- **Low-pressure sharing:** Post photos without worrying about engagement metrics or algorithms
- **Authentic connections:** See what friends are up to without performative content
- **Simple interface:** Easy to use without learning new features every month
- **No FOMO:** Platform that doesn't trigger anxiety or comparison
- **Control over experience:** Choose who to follow and see their content chronologically
- **Privacy-friendly:** Less data collection and tracking than major platforms

### Pain Points
- **Performance anxiety:** Feels pressure to post "perfect" content on Instagram
- **Time sink:** Gets sucked into endless scrolling on algorithm-driven feeds
- **Comparison trap:** Feels bad seeing heavily curated content from influencers
- **Too many features:** Confused by Stories, Highlights, Reels, Shopping tabs
- **Privacy concerns:** Uncomfortable with amount of data Instagram/Meta collects
- **Toxic comments:** Tired of dealing with negative comments on posts

### Typical Tasks
- Upload photos when something interesting happens (travel, events, outings)
- Check feed briefly to see what friends posted
- Like friends' photos to show support
- Follow new friends or acquaintances
- Update profile picture occasionally

### User Journey
1. **Discovery:** Friend recommends inchagram as "social media without the stress"
2. **Onboarding:** Signs up quickly with email, chooses username
3. **Connection:** Follows friends who are already on the platform
4. **First post:** Uploads photo from recent trip without overthinking caption
5. **Engagement:** Checks feed casually, likes friends' posts
6. **Habit:** Uses inchagram as relaxing alternative to main social media apps

### Quote
> "I love that I can just post a photo and not worry about writing the perfect caption, adding hashtags, or whether the algorithm will show it to my friends. It's refreshing."

### Feature Priorities
- **Must Have:** Easy photo upload, chronological feed, following friends
- **Should Have:** Simple profile editing, discover new users
- **Won't Use:** Advanced editing tools, analytics, monetization features

## Morgan - The Mindful Consumer

**Role:** Digital Wellness Advocate
**Age:** 35
**Technical Proficiency:** Intermediate
**Primary Goal:** Enjoy photography content without getting sucked into addictive social media patterns

### Background
Morgan is a yoga instructor and wellness coach who intentionally limits social media use. They appreciate good photography and art but are hyper-aware of how platforms are designed to maximize engagement and time-on-site. Morgan deleted Instagram and TikTok to protect their mental health but misses the ability to discover beautiful photography. They're looking for a platform that respects their time and attention.

### Needs & Motivations
- **Intentional usage:** Platform that doesn't use dark patterns to keep them scrolling
- **No infinite scroll tricks:** Clear stopping points, no auto-play or endless recommendations
- **Quality content:** Curated feed based on who they choose to follow
- **Respect for time:** Quick to check, easy to close
- **Ad-free experience:** No sponsored content or algorithmic manipulation
- **Mental health friendly:** Design that doesn't trigger comparison or anxiety

### Pain Points
- **Addictive design:** Platforms designed to maximize time-on-site at expense of wellbeing
- **Loss of agency:** Algorithms decide what content to show
- **Attention manipulation:** Notifications, badges, streaks designed to create compulsion
- **Surveillance:** Uncomfortable with data collection and targeted advertising
- **Fake engagement:** Meaningless likes and comments that don't foster real connection
- **FOMO triggers:** Stories that disappear, fear of missing out on content

### Typical Tasks
- Check feed intentionally 1-2 times per day for 5-10 minutes
- Follow photographers whose work brings joy
- Like photos mindfully (only content they genuinely appreciate)
- Occasionally share photos from nature walks or travel
- Curate following list to maintain positive feed experience

### User Journey
1. **Discovery:** Reads about inchagram in digital wellness blog as "anti-addictive social media"
2. **Trial:** Signs up with skepticism but open mind
3. **Observation:** Notes lack of infinite scroll triggers, algorithmic suggestions, notification spam
4. **Trust Building:** Appreciates that feed is chronological and respects their choices
5. **Adoption:** Replaces Instagram with inchagram for photography consumption
6. **Advocacy:** Recommends to therapy clients looking for healthier social media alternatives

### Quote
> "Finally, a platform that treats me like a human with agency, not a metric to be maximized. I can enjoy photography without feeling manipulated into endless scrolling."

### Feature Priorities
- **Must Have:** Chronological feed (no algorithm), simple following/unfollowing, no notifications
- **Should Have:** Clear visual hierarchy, no auto-play, opt-in features only
- **Won't Accept:** Infinite scroll, engagement metrics (view counts, like visibility), algorithmic recommendations

## Sam - The Privacy-Conscious User

**Role:** Software Developer
**Age:** 31
**Technical Proficiency:** Expert
**Primary Goal:** Share and view photos on a platform with minimal data collection and tracking

### Background
Sam is a backend developer at a tech company who is very aware of how social media companies monetize user data. They've read Meta's privacy policies and are uncomfortable with the level of data collection, ad targeting, and third-party sharing. Sam wants to share photos with friends and family but refuses to use platforms that treat users as products. They value open-source software and privacy-respecting services.

### Needs & Motivations
- **Data privacy:** Minimal data collection, no ad targeting or third-party sharing
- **Transparency:** Clear privacy policy, no hidden tracking
- **User control:** Ability to export data, delete account permanently
- **No surveillance capitalism:** Platform not built around selling user attention to advertisers
- **Simple permissions:** App doesn't request excessive phone permissions
- **Local-first when possible:** Prefer platforms that don't require cloud sync for everything

### Pain Points
- **Data exploitation:** Uncomfortable with how Meta/Google monetize personal data
- **Opaque policies:** Privacy policies that are deliberately confusing
- **Forced consent:** "Accept all cookies" or can't use the service
- **Shadow profiles:** Platforms tracking non-users and building profiles
- **Behavioral tracking:** Algorithms analyzing behavior to influence and manipulate
- **Data breaches:** Large platforms are targets for hackers due to centralized data

### Typical Tasks
- Review privacy settings after sign-up
- Read terms of service and privacy policy carefully
- Upload photos occasionally, always reviewing what metadata is shared
- Check for new privacy-related features or policy changes
- Recommend platform to friends if it respects privacy principles

### User Journey
1. **Discovery:** Finds inchagram through Hacker News discussion on privacy-respecting alternatives
2. **Research:** Reviews privacy policy, checks if open-source, looks for red flags
3. **Sign-up:** Creates account with minimal information required
4. **Testing:** Uploads test photo, reviews what data is collected
5. **Evaluation:** Monitors network traffic, checks permissions requested
6. **Adoption:** If satisfied with privacy practices, migrates from Instagram
7. **Evangelism:** Writes blog post about privacy-respecting social media alternatives

### Quote
> "I shouldn't have to choose between sharing photos with friends and protecting my privacy. If inchagram respects my data, I'll support it and recommend it to others."

### Feature Priorities
- **Must Have:** Clear privacy policy, minimal data collection, HTTPS, data export
- **Should Have:** Open API, self-hosting option (future), end-to-end encryption (future)
- **Won't Accept:** Ads, third-party trackers, behavioral profiling, selling user data

## Taylor - The Visual Curator

**Role:** Art Gallery Assistant / Creative Professional
**Age:** 26
**Technical Proficiency:** Intermediate-High
**Primary Goal:** Discover and curate collections of inspiring photography and visual art

### Background
Taylor works at an independent art gallery and has a strong eye for composition, color, and visual storytelling. They use social media primarily as a discovery tool for new artists and photographers. Taylor spends more time browsing than posting, and values platforms that surface quality content without algorithmic interference. They're frustrated by Instagram's shift from chronological feeds to engagement-based recommendations.

### Needs & Motivations
- **Content discovery:** Find new photographers and visual artists
- **Curation:** Build a feed of high-quality photography
- **Chronological browsing:** See content in order posted, not algorithmic ranking
- **Explore features:** Discover new accounts without algorithmic manipulation
- **Visual-first experience:** Photos are the star, not text or engagement metrics
- **Support artists:** Platform that helps creators get visibility without paid promotion

### Pain Points
- **Algorithm chaos:** Instagram shows popular/sponsored content instead of artists they follow
- **Engagement gaming:** Quality work buried while viral content surfaces
- **Discovery challenges:** Hard to find new artists without hashtag spam
- **Mixed content types:** Feed cluttered with Reels, Stories, Shopping when they only want photos
- **Chronology loss:** Can't browse work chronologically to see artist's evolution
- **Pay-to-play:** Feels like artists must pay for ads to get visibility

### Typical Tasks
- Browse discover feed to find new photographers
- Follow accounts that post consistently high-quality work
- Like photos to bookmark favorites and show appreciation
- Check feed multiple times daily to see new work from followed artists
- Occasionally share work from gallery exhibitions

### User Journey
1. **Discovery:** Sees inchagram mentioned by photographers on Twitter as better discovery platform
2. **Exploration:** Creates account and spends first hour browsing discover feed
3. **Curation:** Follows 50+ photographers across various styles (landscape, portrait, street, abstract)
4. **Routine:** Checks feed morning and evening to see new work
5. **Discovery habit:** Weekly browse of discover feed to find new artists
6. **Advocacy:** Recommends to gallery artists as platform for visibility

### Quote
> "I love that I see every photo from artists I follow in the order they posted it. No algorithm deciding what's 'relevant' to me based on engagement metrics."

### Feature Priorities
- **Must Have:** Discover feed, chronological following feed, follow/unfollow
- **Should Have:** Save/bookmark photos (future), search by username
- **Would Love:** Collections/folders (future), artist profiles with statement

## Cross-Persona Insights

### Universal Needs
- **Simplicity:** All personas value simplicity over feature complexity
- **Control:** Users want agency over their experience (chronological feed, manual follows)
- **Respect:** Platform that respects time, attention, privacy, and choice
- **Photo-first:** Focus on photography without distraction from videos or other content types

### Universal Pain Points
- **Algorithm fatigue:** All personas frustrated with algorithmic feed manipulation
- **Feature bloat:** Nobody wants more features - they want less complexity
- **Engagement pressure:** Users tired of platforms optimized for engagement metrics over user experience
- **Privacy concerns:** Growing awareness and concern about data practices

### Design Principles Derived from Personas
1. **Less is more:** Resist feature creep, maintain simplicity
2. **User control:** Chronological feeds, manual curation, no algorithmic interference
3. **Respect boundaries:** No dark patterns, notification spam, or time manipulation
4. **Privacy by default:** Minimal data collection, transparent policies
5. **Photo-centric:** Keep focus on photography, not engagement metrics

## Using These Personas

When writing user stories or designing features, reference these personas:

**Format:** "As [Persona Name], I want to [action] so that [benefit]."

**Examples:**
- "As **Alex** (Photography Enthusiast), I want to upload multiple photos at once so that I can share my best shots from a photo walk efficiently."
- "As **Morgan** (Mindful Consumer), I want a chronological feed with clear visual boundaries so that I can browse intentionally without getting sucked into endless scrolling."
- "As **Sam** (Privacy-Conscious), I want to review and control what metadata is shared when uploading photos so that I can protect my privacy."

**Anti-Patterns to Avoid:**
- Features that would frustrate **Morgan** with addictive design
- Complexity that would overwhelm **Jamie** looking for simplicity
- Privacy violations that would cause **Sam** to leave the platform
- Algorithm manipulation that would annoy **Alex** and **Taylor**
